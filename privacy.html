Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 July 2026

Solyra is a guided visualization practice built around things you tell us about your life. We treat that as what it is: personal. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

The short version. We collect what we need to make your meditations and run your subscription. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share your written vision with anyone except the AI providers who generate your audio. You can delete everything from inside the app.

What we collect

  • What you write during onboarding. Your first name, the focus areas you choose, your description of your future, what you want to release, your preferred voice, session length, and reminder time. This is the material your meditations are made from.
  • Your practice activity. Sessions completed, streaks, challenge progress, and any reflections you save after a session.
  • Subscription status. Whether you have an active membership and which tier. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Diagnostics and usage. Anonymous app-performance and screen-flow data, so we can see where the app is confusing or broken. Not linked to your identity.

What we do with it

  • Generate your personalized meditation scripts and audio.
  • Keep your practice, streak, and challenge progress in sync.
  • Send the daily reminder you asked for, at the time you chose.
  • Provide support when you contact us, and fix defects.

That is the complete list. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use your vision text to train our own models.

Who else sees your data

To create your meditations we send the relevant parts of your onboarding answers to AI providers who generate the script and the narration. They process it to produce your audio and are contractually restricted from using it for their own purposes.

  • Anthropic — writes your meditation script from your answers.
  • ElevenLabs — narrates the script in the voice you selected.
  • Supabase — hosts your account, practice data, and generated audio.
  • Apple — processes subscriptions and, if you allow it, delivers notifications.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

How long we keep it

Your account data stays until you delete it. Delete your account in the app and your profile, vision text, reflections, and generated audio are removed from our systems within 30 days, including backups. Anonymous diagnostics may remain, since they cannot be traced back to you.

Your choices

  • See it. Everything you have written is visible in the app.
  • Change it. Edit your vision, focus areas, voice, and reminders at any time in Settings.
  • Delete it. Settings has a delete-account option that removes your data permanently.
  • Turn off notifications. In the app, or in iOS Settings.
  • Ask us anything. Email privacy@getsolyra.com and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have rights to access, correct, port, and erase your data, and to object to processing. If you are in California, you have the right to know what we collect and to request deletion, and we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA. The same controls above cover all of these; email us if you would rather we handle it directly.

Children

Solyra is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production systems is limited to those who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but your vision text is treated as sensitive and handled accordingly.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@getsolyra.com

Solyra is a guided visualization and meditation practice. It is not medical or mental-health care, and it makes no promises about life outcomes.
